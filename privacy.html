2.4 Contact form

The contact form on the Contact page does not send anything to our servers. It opens a pre-filled email in your own email app (company name, contact name, work email, target market, services of interest, project details); we receive it as an ordinary email and keep it in our mailbox.

2.5 Automatically collected technical data

  • Server and hosting logs (IP address, time, requested URL, browser type) kept by the hosting providers listed in section 4 for security and troubleshooting.
  • Rate-limiting records: short-lived counters keyed by IP address and/or email to block brute-force login and code-guessing attempts. They expire automatically within twice the limit window (at most a few hours).
  • Browser storage: see the Cookie & Storage Policy. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

4. Who we share it with

4.1 Brand partners

Before you apply: approved brand partners can search Vively creators from their dashboard. For each matching creator they see: username, full name, profile picture, bio (first 160 characters), content categories, nationality, country of residence, gender, age in years, Instagram/TikTok/YouTube handles, self-reported follower counts, and a match score with short reasons. Your email address and phone number are not shown at this stage.

When you apply to a campaign, the brand that owns it also receives your email address, phone number and any message you wrote. Brands agree in our Terms of Service to use this information only for that campaign.

4.2 Vively staff

Vively administrators can view creator and brand accounts, applications and uploaded documents to operate and review the service.

8. Security

  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Login sessions use signed tokens that expire after 7 days.
  • Sensitive account changes (profile edits, account deletion) require a one-time code sent to your email.
  • Login, code and password-reset endpoints are rate-limited to slow down guessing attacks.
  • Business registration certificates are stored as private assets that are not reachable at a public URL.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

9. Age requirement

You must be at least 17 years old to create a Vively creator account; sign-up asks for your date of birth and a confirmation, and accounts that do not meet the requirement are rejected. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17. If you believe a younger person has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
